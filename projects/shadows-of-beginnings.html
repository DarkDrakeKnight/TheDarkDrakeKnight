Summary

Shadows of Beginnings is a top-down action RPG developed as a team project. My primary focus was level design, with an emphasis on player movement, encounter pacing, exploration, and creating spaces that support the tone of the game.

This project is a strong example of my ability to work with others and contribute to a shared vision. It also shows my interest in designing spaces that support both gameplay and atmosphere.

Project Breakdown

  • Engine: Unreal Engine
  • Team Size: 4 developers
  • My Role: Level Design, blockouts, pacing, encounter support, Rogue class setup and design
  • Project Type: Educational team project
  • Tools Used: Unreal Engine, Git/GitHub, Blender

Preproduction

Before building the level spaces, I worked through planning and layout ideas to make sure the areas supported the player experience. This included rough top-down sketches, randomized travel logic prototypes, and references for the overall mood of the game.

I also used planning visuals such as simple blockout sketches to think through how the player would move through each space and where the game should build pressure, slow down, or reward exploration.

Reflection

This project helped me improve my understanding of how level layout affects pacing and tension. One of the biggest lessons was that a level is not just a space to move through, but a tool for guiding the player’s decisions. Going forward, I want to keep improving how I document my design process and present my work more clearly.
