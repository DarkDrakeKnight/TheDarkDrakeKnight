About Me

Welcome to my forge. I am a game development student at Full Sail University with a strong interest in interactive systems, gameplay programming, and creative worldbuilding. I enjoy learning through hands-on projects and improving my skills one build at a time.

My background includes 13 years of active duty service in the U.S. Air Force as a maintenance craftsman. That experience helped me develop discipline, adaptability, problem-solving, teamwork, and attention to detail. Those strengths carry over into game development and help me stay focused when projects become challenging.

I like combining a gamer mindset with a technical approach. Whether I am working in Unity, Vulkan, or Unreal, I am always looking for ways to create polished, functional, and engaging experiences for players.
